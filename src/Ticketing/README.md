# Ticketing

Tickets from the first report to done: keys, moves, assignment, lead checks and duplicate links. It owns the tables `tkt_project`, `tkt_ticket`, `tkt_ticket_event`, `tkt_duplicate_link` and `tkt_summary`. Its Api is `ProjectQueries`, `ProjectSummary`, `ProjectChoice`, `ProjectPermission` and `TicketVisibility` with `TicketVisibilityFacts` and `TicketVisibilitySql` (built; the one ticket visibility predicate and its SQL form), `TicketVisibilities` (built; a viewer's predicate, implemented by `Application\RoleTicketVisibilities`), `TicketCommands` (built: `create`; `Application\UseCaseTicketCommands` hands it to the use case `Application\CreateTicket\CreateTicket`, which refuses an assignee who is not a lead or member of the project's team with `AssigneeNotInTeam`, a blank title with `TicketTitleMissing`, a title over 200 characters with `TicketTitleOverLimit` and a description over 50,000 with `TicketDescriptionOverLimit` (for these three the number is already taken, and only the caller's rollback gives it back) and writes the ticket, its `created` history row, its turn row and `TicketCreated` in the caller's transaction; the other commands still to come), `TicketTurnQueries` (built; `factsOf` gives Insight's turn source one ticket with its step and its team's lead, lead's name and people, `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalTicketTurnQueries`), `TicketQueries` (built in P03.T3: `list`; steps come from Workflow's cached `StepQueries::listedSteps`, turn users from Insight's `TurnUserQueries::turnUsersOf`, names from Identity's `UserQueries::byIds`, one batch each; built in P03.T6: `pageOf(string $key, TicketVisibility $viewer): ?TicketPageView`, one ticket only when the viewer may see it, its parent's key only when they may see the parent, in two queries; `positionOf(string $key, ListFilter): ?TicketPosition`, the ticket's "N of M" and neighbours in the list's own order in one query, by keyset and with the list's capped counts), plus its public events. It may use the Api of Workflow, Organization, Identity, Media and Insight. Its Domain has the `Ticket` aggregate (mapped to `tkt_ticket`; `Ticket::report` starts a ticket in To do with an assignee, else in Backlog), `TicketKey`, `TicketType`, `Priority` and `Visibility`; `tkt_summary` has no entity yet. The tables `tkt_ticket`, `tkt_ticket_event` and `tkt_summary` are built (`Version20261111120000`, `Version20261111140000`). `tkt_ticket_event` is partitioned by month, with partitions through 2027-12 from `Version20261111150000`; `Infrastructure\Scheduler\AddTicketEventPartitions` adds them daily, three months ahead, and the health check `next_ticket_event_partition` fails when next month's is missing. The job is `#[WhenNot('prod')]` (prod has no migrator connection), so prod needs a runner as `pinround_migrator` before 2027-12 ([phase-03 hand-off notes](../../docs/05-plan/phase-03-tickets-core.md#hand-off-notes)). It implements Workflow's `StepOccupancy` (`Infrastructure\Workflow\TicketStepOccupancy`), so Workflow learns how many open tickets are in a step without calling Ticketing; it counts the step's tickets with `closed_at IS NULL` of the tenant (by `organization_id` and row-level security), as `Infrastructure\Doctrine\TicketOpenTicketCounts::openTicketsIn` does per project; both refuse with no transaction open or no tenant set rather than count 0. These two are guards (`RemoveStep`, `UnlinkProject`) and count every open ticket, whoever may see it; never show them. The counts a person is shown come from `OpenTicketCounts::openTicketsVisibleTo(TicketVisibility, ProjectId ...)`, the same one query for all projects filtered with `TicketVisibilitySql` (ADR-0013 "manager counts"): `ProjectQueries::projectsOf(TeamId, UserId $viewerId)` builds the viewer's predicate with `TicketVisibilities::of`, so the Team page counts only the tickets its viewer may see. The Team page's project actions are its POST routes `ticketing_team_projects_unlink`, `ticketing_team_projects_link` and `ticketing_team_projects_create` under `/teams/{slug}/projects` (`UI\TeamProjectsController`); each runs in a transaction of its own through SharedKernel's port `Application\Transactions`. The new ticket form is `GET`/`POST /tickets/new` (`ticketing_ticket_new`, `UI\NewTicketController`), also in a transaction of its own, so a refused create gives its number back: it offers the projects of the actor's teams in which `TicketPermission::CREATE` is granted (`ProjectQueries::projectChoicesOfTeams`, one query, as `TeamProjectChoice` with id, key, name and team id; a project no team owns is never offered), Bug and Normal preselected, and, only for a Lead of an offered project's team (as `TicketPermission::ASSIGN`), "Give it to" with the people of the teams they lead, grouped by team name, from Organization's `TeamQueries::possibleAssigneesOf` and `summariesOf` and Identity's `UserQueries::byIds`. A person with no such project gets 403, and so does a POST of another project, of one that was removed or lost its team before the save (`ProjectNotFound`, `ProjectHasNoTeam`), or of an assignee from someone who does not lead the project's team; field and assignee refusals answer 422 with the form, and a created ticket redirects to its page `/t/{key}` with the toast "Ticket SHOP-n made.". The ticket page is `GET /t/{key}` (`ticket_show`, `UI\TicketShowController`, `@Ticketing/show.html.twig`): it reads `TicketQueries::pageOf` and checks `TicketPermission::VIEW` on the page's visibility facts as a second guard; an unknown, malformed, other organization's or hidden ticket is the same 404. Each row of the tickets list links to its page with the list's filters (`project`, `person`, `done`, `q`, `team`), which `UI\TicketListContext` reads and checks for both pages; on the ticket page a filter the list would refuse only drops the context, and "N of M" with Previous and Next walk that list or, failing it, the plain open-tickets list. The demo seed `Infrastructure\Demo\SeedDemoTickets` (after `SeedDemoProjects`) makes 40 SHOP tickets, five in each core step, through `TicketCommands::create` and then one UPDATE that sets the step (no move command exists before P03.T7); a title SHOP already holds is kept, so a second run adds none. Tests make tickets with `tests/Factory/Ticketing/TicketFactory`.

The ticket page's Now and Next lines (`UI\NowNextSentences`) read the turn facts from `TicketTurnQueries`, the waiting turns from Insight's `TeamLoadQueries` and the sentences from Workflow's `TurnRules` and `NowNext`.
