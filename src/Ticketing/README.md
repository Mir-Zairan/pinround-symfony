# Ticketing

Tickets from the first report to done: keys, moves, assignment, lead checks and duplicate links. It owns the tables `tkt_project`, `tkt_ticket`, `tkt_ticket_event`, `tkt_duplicate_link` and `tkt_summary`. Its Api is `ProjectQueries`, `ProjectSummary`, `ProjectChoice`, `ProjectPermission` and `TicketVisibility` with `TicketVisibilityFacts` and `TicketVisibilitySql` (built; the one ticket visibility predicate and its SQL form), `TicketVisibilities` (built; a viewer's predicate, implemented by `Application\RoleTicketVisibilities`), `TicketCommands` (built: `create`, `move` and `moveTo`; `Application\UseCaseTicketCommands` hands `create` to the use case `Application\CreateTicket\CreateTicket`, which refuses an assignee who is not a lead or member of the project's team with `AssigneeNotInTeam`, a blank title with `TicketTitleMissing`, a title over 200 characters with `TicketTitleOverLimit` and a description over 50,000 with `TicketDescriptionOverLimit` (for these three the number is already taken, and only the caller's rollback gives it back) and writes the ticket, its `created` history row, its turn row and `TicketCreated` in the caller's transaction; it hands `move` (by a Workflow verb) and `moveTo` (the lead's "Move to another step…", no verb) to `Application\MoveTicket\MoveTicket`, which loads the ticket by key at the caller's version (`TicketNotFound`, `TicketChangedMeanwhile`), reads its team's steps and people once as `MoveTeam`, builds the snapshot before the move with `TicketTurnSnapshots::ofTeamTicket` from them, asks Workflow's `TransitionPolicy` and refuses with `MoveRefused` (the rule's note when one explains it), also for a ticket no team owns, `give_it_to` (P03.T8), a waiting-on given for a step other than Needs input, or a person to wait on who is no member of the organization, refuses both a person and a label to wait on, or a label that is blank, over 100 characters or not one line, with `\InvalidArgumentException`, and writes the ticket under its version lock, its `moved` history row, its turn row and project count, and `TicketMoved` (with the verb, or null for `moveTo`) in the caller's transaction; in Needs input the ticket waits on the person or label named, else its reporter; the other commands still to come), `TicketTurnQueries` (built; `factsOf` gives Insight's turn source one ticket with its step and its team's lead, lead's name and people, `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalTicketTurnQueries`, one query when the team's people are cached and three on a miss; the people come from Organization's shared `TeamPeopleQueries` cache, so a reader that read the old rows just before a `SetTeamRole` commit and saved them just after its after-commit clear keeps them cached until the relayed `TeamRoleChanged` is handled in worker-notify (at worst, when `InvalidateTeamPeopleCacheHandler` runs out of retries and the event lands in `messenger_failed`, until the entry's one-day lifetime runs out or the failed message is retried), and a turn row saved meanwhile keeps the old people until the ticket's next refresh; see modules.md), `TicketQueries` (built in P03.T3: `list`; steps come from Workflow's cached `StepQueries::listedSteps`, turn users from Insight's `TurnUserQueries::turnUsersOf`, names from Identity's `UserQueries::byIds`, one batch each; built in P03.T6: `pageOf(string $key, TicketVisibility $viewer): ?TicketPageView`, one ticket only when the viewer may see it, its parent's key only when they may see the parent, in two queries when the team's people are cached (the ticket, the names; the team's lead and people come from Organization's cached `TeamPeopleQueries`, two more on a miss); `positionOf(string $key, ListFilter): ?TicketPosition`, the ticket's "N of M" and neighbours in the list's own order in one query, by keyset and with the list's capped counts), plus its public events. It may use the Api of Workflow, Organization, Identity, Media and Insight. Its Domain has the `Ticket` aggregate (mapped to `tkt_ticket`; `Ticket::report` starts a ticket in To do with an assignee, else in Backlog; `Ticket::move` takes the target step with its group, which the caller resolves, keeps the step it left, sets `closed_at` on entering the done group and clears it on leaving, and keeps who it waits on, a `WaitingOn` person or label, only in Needs input, refusing it elsewhere and its lack there with `InvalidWaitingOn`), its repository `Tickets` (`Infrastructure\Doctrine\DoctrineTickets`; `ofKey` loads one by organization and `TicketKey` in one query; callers parse the key with `TicketKey::fromString` and answer its `InvalidTicketKey` as not found; `save` flushes a loaded ticket under Doctrine's optimistic lock on `version` and turns a stale save into the Domain's `TicketVersionConflict`; the Application port `TicketHistory::recordMoved` writes the `moved` row with the steps, actor and a payload of only the given `verb` and `override_note`; for the move commands the Api has `TicketNotFound`, `TicketChangedMeanwhile`, `MoveRefused` (the refusing rule's note as template and people, as this Api may not hold Workflow's `RuleNote`) and the event `TicketMoved`), `TicketKey`, `TicketType`, `Priority` and `Visibility`; its Application port `TicketTurnSnapshots::of(Ticket)` (`Infrastructure\Doctrine\EntityTicketTurnSnapshots`) builds from a loaded ticket the snapshot `TicketTurnQueries::factsOf` would read from its saved row, through the same `TicketTeamLookup`, and throws rather than give a snapshot for a step or team the organization lacks; `TicketTurnSnapshots::ofTeamTicket(Ticket, $teamSteps, $teamPeople)` builds the same from a team's steps and people the caller has already read, so a move reads no cache twice; `tkt_summary` has no entity yet. The tables `tkt_ticket`, `tkt_ticket_event` and `tkt_summary` are built (`Version20261111120000`, `Version20261111140000`). `tkt_ticket_event` is partitioned by month, with partitions through 2027-12 from `Version20261111150000`; `Infrastructure\Scheduler\AddTicketEventPartitions` adds them daily, three months ahead, and the health check `next_ticket_event_partition` fails when next month's is missing. The job is `#[WhenNot('prod')]` (prod has no migrator connection), so prod needs a runner as `pinround_migrator` before 2027-12 ([phase-03 hand-off notes](../../docs/05-plan/phase-03-tickets-core.md#hand-off-notes)). It implements Workflow's `StepOccupancy` (`Infrastructure\Workflow\TicketStepOccupancy`), so Workflow learns how many open tickets are in a step without calling Ticketing; it counts the step's tickets with `closed_at IS NULL` of the tenant (by `organization_id` and row-level security), as `Infrastructure\Doctrine\TicketOpenTicketCounts::openTicketsIn` does per project; both refuse with no transaction open or no tenant set rather than count 0. These two are guards (`RemoveStep`, `UnlinkProject`) and count every open ticket, whoever may see it; never show them. The counts a person is shown come from `OpenTicketCounts::openTicketsVisibleTo(TicketVisibility, ProjectId ...)`, the same one query for all projects filtered with `TicketVisibilitySql` (ADR-0013 "manager counts"): `ProjectQueries::projectsOf(TeamId, UserId $viewerId)` builds the viewer's predicate with `TicketVisibilities::of`, so the Team page counts only the tickets its viewer may see. The Team page's project actions are its POST routes `ticketing_team_projects_unlink`, `ticketing_team_projects_link` and `ticketing_team_projects_create` under `/teams/{slug}/projects` (`UI\TeamProjectsController`); each runs in a transaction of its own through SharedKernel's port `Application\Transactions`. The new ticket form is `GET`/`POST /tickets/new` (`ticketing_ticket_new`, `UI\NewTicketController`), also in a transaction of its own, so a refused create gives its number back: it offers the projects of the actor's teams in which `TicketPermission::CREATE` is granted (`ProjectQueries::projectChoicesOfTeams`, one query, as `TeamProjectChoice` with id, key, name and team id; a project no team owns is never offered), Bug and Normal preselected, and, only for a Lead of an offered project's team (as `TicketPermission::ASSIGN`), "Give it to" with the people of the teams they lead, grouped by team name, from Organization's `TeamQueries::possibleAssigneesOf` and `summariesOf` and Identity's `UserQueries::byIds`. A person with no such project gets 403, and so does a POST of another project, of one that was removed or lost its team before the save (`ProjectNotFound`, `ProjectHasNoTeam`), or of an assignee from someone who does not lead the project's team; field and assignee refusals answer 422 with the form, and a created ticket redirects to its page `/t/{key}` with the toast "Ticket SHOP-n made.". The ticket page is `GET /t/{key}` (`ticket_show`, `UI\TicketShowController`, `@Ticketing/show.html.twig`): it reads `TicketQueries::pageOf` and checks `TicketPermission::VIEW` on the page's visibility facts as a second guard; an unknown, malformed, other organization's or hidden ticket is the same 404. Each row of the tickets list links to its page with the list's filters (`project`, `person`, `done`, `q`, `team`), which `UI\TicketListContext` reads and checks for both pages; on the ticket page a filter the list would refuse only drops the context, and "N of M" with Previous and Next walk that list or, failing it, the plain open-tickets list. The demo seed `Infrastructure\Demo\SeedDemoTickets` (after `SeedDemoProjects`) makes 41 SHOP tickets, five in each core step plus one in Backlog with an ask past 700 characters that the ticket page's e2e spec opens, through `TicketCommands::create` and then one UPDATE that sets the step rather than `TicketCommands::move`, skipping the transition policy and the `moved` history row on purpose and setting what no move sets (visibility, reviewer, tester, the label On hold waits on); a title SHOP already holds is kept, so a second run adds none. Tests make tickets with `tests/Factory/Ticketing/TicketFactory`.

The ticket page's Now and Next lines (`UI\NowNextSentences`) read the turn snapshot from `TicketQueries::pageOf` (`TicketPageView`), the saved turn and waiting turns from Insight's `TeamLoadQueries` and the sentences from Workflow's `TurnRules` and `NowNext`; `pageTurnOf` gives that read as a `UI\PageTurn`, which `linesOf` words and `UI\NextStepChoices` turns into the "Your next step" box's `UI\NextStep` (the sentence, Workflow's `TransitionPolicy::available` verbs and rule note, and the lead's `moveChoices` over `StepQueries::moveTargetsFor`).
