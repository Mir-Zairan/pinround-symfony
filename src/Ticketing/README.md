# Ticketing

Tickets from the first report to done: keys, moves, assignment, lead checks and duplicate links. It owns the tables `tkt_project`, `tkt_ticket`, `tkt_ticket_event`, `tkt_duplicate_link` and `tkt_summary`. Its Api is `ProjectQueries`, `ProjectSummary`, `ProjectChoice`, `ProjectPermission` and `TicketVisibility` with `TicketVisibilityFacts` and `TicketVisibilitySql` (built; the one ticket visibility predicate and its SQL form), `TicketVisibilities` (built; a viewer's predicate, implemented by `Application\RoleTicketVisibilities`), `TicketCommands` (built: `create`; `Application\UseCaseTicketCommands` hands it to the use case `Application\CreateTicket\CreateTicket`, which refuses an assignee who is not a lead or member of the project's team with `AssigneeNotInTeam`, a blank title with `TicketTitleMissing`, a title over 200 characters with `TicketTitleOverLimit` and a description over 50,000 with `TicketDescriptionOverLimit` (for these three the number is already taken, and only the caller's rollback gives it back) and writes the ticket, its `created` history row, its turn row and `TicketCreated` in the caller's transaction; the other commands still to come), `TicketTurnQueries` (built; `factsOf` gives Insight's turn source one ticket with its step and its team's lead, lead's name and people, `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalTicketTurnQueries`), `TicketQueries` (still to come), plus its public events. It may use the Api of Workflow, Organization, Identity, Media and Insight. Its Domain has the `Ticket` aggregate (mapped to `tkt_ticket`; `Ticket::report` starts a ticket in To do with an assignee, else in Backlog), `TicketKey`, `TicketType`, `Priority` and `Visibility`; `tkt_summary` has no entity yet. The tables `tkt_ticket`, `tkt_ticket_event` and `tkt_summary` are built (`Version20261111120000`, `Version20261111140000`). `tkt_ticket_event` is partitioned by month, with partitions through 2027-12 from `Version20261111150000`; `Infrastructure\Scheduler\AddTicketEventPartitions` adds them daily, three months ahead, and the health check `next_ticket_event_partition` fails when next month's is missing. The job is `#[WhenNot('prod')]` (prod has no migrator connection), so prod needs a runner as `pinround_migrator` before 2027-12 ([phase-03 hand-off notes](../../docs/05-plan/phase-03-tickets-core.md#hand-off-notes)). It implements Workflow's `StepOccupancy` (`Infrastructure\Workflow\TicketStepOccupancy`), so Workflow learns how many open tickets are in a step without calling Ticketing; it counts the step's tickets with `closed_at IS NULL` of the tenant (by `organization_id` and row-level security), as `Infrastructure\Doctrine\TicketOpenTicketCounts` does per project; both refuse with no transaction open or no tenant set rather than count 0. The Team page's project actions are its POST routes `ticketing_team_projects_unlink`, `ticketing_team_projects_link` and `ticketing_team_projects_create` under `/teams/{slug}/projects` (`UI\TeamProjectsController`); each runs in a transaction of its own through SharedKernel's port `Application\Transactions`. The new ticket form is `GET`/`POST /tickets/new` (`ticketing_ticket_new`, `UI\NewTicketController`), also in a transaction of its own, so a refused create gives its number back: it offers the projects of the actor's teams in which `TicketPermission::CREATE` is granted (`ProjectQueries::projectChoicesOfTeams`, one query, as `TeamProjectChoice` with id, key, name and team id; a project no team owns is never offered), Bug and Normal preselected, and "Give it to" grouped by project from Organization's `TeamQueries::possibleAssigneesOf` and Identity's `UserQueries::byIds`. A person with no such project gets 403, and so does a POST of another project, or of one that lost its team before the save; field and assignee refusals answer 422 with the form, and a created ticket redirects to the ticket list of its project with the toast "Ticket SHOP-n made.".
