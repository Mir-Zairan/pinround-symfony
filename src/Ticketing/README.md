# Ticketing

Tickets from the first report to done: keys, moves, assignment, lead checks and duplicate links. It owns the tables `tkt_project`, `tkt_ticket`, `tkt_ticket_event`, `tkt_duplicate_link` and `tkt_summary`. Its Api is `ProjectQueries`, `ProjectSummary`, `ProjectChoice`, `ProjectPermission` and `TicketVisibility` with `TicketVisibilityFacts` and `TicketVisibilitySql` (built; the one ticket visibility predicate and its SQL form), `TicketVisibilities` (built; a viewer's predicate, implemented by `Application\RoleTicketVisibilities`), `TicketCommands` (built: `create`, `move`, `moveTo` and `giveTo`; `Application\UseCaseTicketCommands` hands `create` to the use case `Application\CreateTicket\CreateTicket`, which refuses an assignee who is not a lead or member of the project's team with `AssigneeNotInTeam`, a blank title with `TicketTitleMissing`, a title over 200 characters with `TicketTitleOverLimit` and a description over 50,000 with `TicketDescriptionOverLimit` (for these three the number is already taken, and only the caller's rollback gives it back) and writes the ticket, its `created` history row, its turn row and `TicketCreated` in the caller's transaction; it hands `move` (by a Workflow verb), `moveTo` (the lead's "Move to another step…", no verb) and `giveTo` (the `give_it_to` verb with its person) to `Application\MoveTicket\MoveTicket`, which loads the ticket by key at the caller's version (`TicketNotFound`, `TicketChangedMeanwhile`), reads its team's steps and people once as `MoveTeam`, builds the snapshot before the move with `TicketTurnSnapshots::ofTeamTicket` from them, asks Workflow's `TransitionPolicy` and refuses with `MoveRefused` (the rule's note when one explains it), also for a ticket no team owns, `give_it_to` through `move` (only `giveTo` takes it), a waiting-on given for a step other than Needs input, or a person to wait on who is no member of the organization, refuses both a person and a label to wait on, or a label that is blank, over 100 characters or not one line, with `\InvalidArgumentException`, and writes the ticket under its version lock, its `moved` history row, its turn row and project count, and `TicketMoved` (with the verb, or null for `moveTo`) in the caller's transaction; `giveTo` makes the person the assignee, also moving a Backlog ticket to To do in the same save, refuses with `MoveRefused` a person who is not the team's lead or a member ("Give it to someone in the ticket’s team.") or who already has it ("{assignee} already has this ticket."), clears the stored reviewer (and the stored tester when it is the new assignee), writes the `moved` row with the payload `verb` and `assignee_id`, and publishes `TicketAssigned`, with `TicketMoved` only for a ticket that left Backlog (the UI route is `POST /t/{key}/assign`, `ticket_assign`, the lead's only); in Needs input the ticket waits on the person or label named, else its reporter; entering the core Code review or Testing step stores the reviewer or tester Workflow's `TurnPeopleChoice` picks from the waiting turns the move read once, when the ticket has none yet (none for the lead-reviews-own-code override or a team with no tester); the other commands still to come), `TicketTurnQueries` (built; `factsOf` gives Insight's turn source one ticket with its step and its team's lead, lead's name and people, `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalTicketTurnQueries`, one query when the team's people are cached and three on a miss; the people come from Organization's shared `TeamPeopleQueries` cache, so a reader that read the old rows just before a `SetTeamRole` commit and saved them just after its after-commit clear keeps them cached until the relayed `TeamRoleChanged` is handled in worker-notify (at worst, when `InvalidateTeamPeopleCacheHandler` runs out of retries and the event lands in `messenger_failed`, until the entry's one-day lifetime runs out or the failed message is retried), and a turn row saved meanwhile keeps the old people until the ticket's next refresh; see modules.md), `TicketQueries` (built in P03.T3: `list`; steps come from Workflow's cached `StepQueries::listedSteps`, turn users from Insight's `TurnUserQueries::turnUsersOf`, names from Identity's `UserQueries::byIds`, one batch each; built in P03.T6: `pageOf(string $key, TicketVisibility $viewer): ?TicketPageView`, one ticket only when the viewer may see it, its parent's key only when they may see the parent, in two queries when the team's people are cached (the ticket, the names; the team's lead and people come from Organization's cached `TeamPeopleQueries`, two more on a miss); `positionOf(string $key, ListFilter): ?TicketPosition`, the ticket's "N of M" and neighbours in the list's own order in one query, by keyset and with the list's capped counts), `TicketPauses` (built in P04.T2 Q1 for Conversation's AskQuestion, Answer and WithdrawQuestion, `Application\LockedTicketPauses`: `pauseForQuestion(TicketId, UserId $askedUserId, UserId $askerId)` sets the question pause, which gives the turn to the asked person and refuses every move until the answer, after checking on the locked row that the asked person is the ticket's assignee, reporter, reviewer or tester or its team's lead (`AskedPersonNotOnTicket` otherwise, nothing saved, so a reassignment saved meanwhile counts), and `endQuestionPause(TicketId)` clears it on an answer or a withdrawal; the step stays. Call it inside an open transaction under the tenant: it locks the ticket's row until that transaction ends, so two asks cannot both pass the check, and saves at once, raising the version but keeping `updated_at`, so the ticket keeps its list place. It throws `TicketNotFound` (no ticket of the organization with the id), `TicketAlreadyPaused` ("This ticket already waits for an answer."), `TicketChangedMeanwhile` (the request loaded the ticket before another request saved it; the caller rolls back), `\InvalidArgumentException` only when the asker, who is on the ticket, asks themselves (an asker not on the ticket who asks themselves gets `AskedPersonNotOnTicket`), and `\LogicException` with no open transaction or no tenant, before any query, or from `endQuestionPause` for a ticket that waits for no answer ("This ticket waits for no answer."). The caller checks who may ask, answer and withdraw and refreshes the turn with Insight's `TurnProjector::refresh` after each call, in the same transaction), `ReviewChangeLabels` with `ReviewedPin` (built in P05; Review implements it, see below), plus its public events. It may use the Api of Workflow, Organization, Identity, Media and Insight. Its Domain has the `Ticket` aggregate (mapped to `tkt_ticket`; `Ticket::report` starts a ticket in To do with an assignee, else in Backlog; `Ticket::move` takes the target step with its group, which the caller resolves, keeps the step it left, sets `closed_at` on entering the done group and clears it on leaving, and keeps who it waits on, a `WaitingOn` person or label, only in Needs input, refusing it elsewhere and its lack there with `InvalidWaitingOn`, and fills an empty reviewer or tester with the one the caller chose, keeping one already stored), its repository `Tickets` (`Infrastructure\Doctrine\DoctrineTickets`; `ofKey` loads one by organization and `TicketKey` in one query; callers parse the key with `TicketKey::fromString` and answer its `InvalidTicketKey` as not found; `save` flushes a loaded ticket under Doctrine's optimistic lock on `version` and turns a stale save into the Domain's `TicketVersionConflict`; `lockedOf` (built in P04.T2 Q1) loads one by organization and id under a row lock (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`), refusing with `\LogicException` outside a transaction, and locks a ticket the request loaded already without reloading it, refusing it with `TicketVersionConflict` when another request saved it since, so no check reads its old fields; the pause columns `asked_user_id` and `asker_id` are both set or both null (`tkt_ticket_question_pause_check`, `Version20261115100000`); the Application port `TicketHistory::recordMoved` writes the `moved` row with the steps, actor and a payload of only the given `verb` and `override_note`, and `TicketHistory::recordGiven` writes a give's `moved` row with the payload `verb` `give_it_to` and, under `TicketHistory::GIVEN_TO_PAYLOAD_KEY` (`assignee_id`), the person given it, which `DbalTicketHistoryEntries` reads back for What happened; for the move commands the Api has `TicketNotFound`, `TicketChangedMeanwhile`, `MoveRefused` (the refusing rule's note as template and people, as this Api may not hold Workflow's `RuleNote`) and the events `TicketMoved` and `TicketAssigned`), `TicketKey`, `TicketType`, `Priority` and `Visibility`; its Application port `TicketTurnSnapshots::of(Ticket)` (`Infrastructure\Doctrine\EntityTicketTurnSnapshots`) builds from a loaded ticket the snapshot `TicketTurnQueries::factsOf` would read from its saved row, through the same `TicketTeamLookup`, and throws rather than give a snapshot for a step or team the organization lacks; `TicketTurnSnapshots::ofTeamTicket(Ticket, $teamSteps, $teamPeople)` builds the same from a team's steps and people the caller has already read, so a move reads no cache twice; `tkt_summary` has no entity yet. The tables `tkt_ticket`, `tkt_ticket_event` and `tkt_summary` are built (`Version20261111120000`, `Version20261111140000`). `tkt_ticket_event` is partitioned by month, with partitions through 2027-12 from `Version20261111150000`; `Infrastructure\Scheduler\AddTicketEventPartitions` adds them daily, three months ahead, and the health check `next_ticket_event_partition` fails when next month's is missing. The job is `#[WhenNot('prod')]` (prod has no migrator connection), so prod needs a runner as `pinround_migrator` before 2027-12 ([phase-03 hand-off notes](../../docs/05-plan/phase-03-tickets-core.md#hand-off-notes)). It implements Workflow's `StepOccupancy` (`Infrastructure\Workflow\TicketStepOccupancy`), so Workflow learns how many open tickets are in a step without calling Ticketing; it counts the step's tickets with `closed_at IS NULL` of the tenant (by `organization_id` and row-level security), as `Infrastructure\Doctrine\TicketOpenTicketCounts::openTicketsIn` does per project; both refuse with no transaction open or no tenant set rather than count 0. These two are guards (`RemoveStep`, `UnlinkProject`) and count every open ticket, whoever may see it; never show them. The counts a person is shown come from `OpenTicketCounts::openTicketsVisibleTo(TicketVisibility, ProjectId ...)`, the same one query for all projects filtered with `TicketVisibilitySql` (ADR-0013 "manager counts"): `ProjectQueries::projectsOf(TeamId, UserId $viewerId)` builds the viewer's predicate with `TicketVisibilities::of`, so the Team page counts only the tickets its viewer may see. The Team page's project actions are its POST routes `ticketing_team_projects_unlink`, `ticketing_team_projects_link` and `ticketing_team_projects_create` under `/teams/{slug}/projects` (`UI\TeamProjectsController`); each runs in a transaction of its own through SharedKernel's port `Application\Transactions`. The new ticket form is `GET`/`POST /tickets/new` (`ticketing_ticket_new`, `UI\NewTicketController`), also in a transaction of its own, so a refused create gives its number back: it offers the projects of the actor's teams in which `TicketPermission::CREATE` is granted (`ProjectQueries::projectChoicesOfTeams`, one query, as `TeamProjectChoice` with id, key, name and team id; a project no team owns is never offered), Bug and Normal preselected, and, only for a Lead of an offered project's team (as `TicketPermission::ASSIGN`), "Give it to" with the people of the teams they lead, grouped by team name, from Organization's `TeamQueries::possibleAssigneesOf` and `summariesOf` and Identity's `UserQueries::byIds`. A person with no such project gets 403, and so does a POST of another project, of one that was removed or lost its team before the save (`ProjectNotFound`, `ProjectHasNoTeam`), or of an assignee from someone who does not lead the project's team; field and assignee refusals answer 422 with the form, and a created ticket redirects to its page `/t/{key}` with the toast "Ticket SHOP-n made.". The ticket page is `GET /t/{key}` (`ticket_show`, `UI\TicketShowController`, `@Ticketing/show.html.twig`): it reads `TicketQueries::pageOf` and checks `TicketPermission::VIEW` on the page's visibility facts as a second guard; an unknown, malformed, other organization's or hidden ticket is the same 404. Each row of the tickets list links to its page with the list's filters (`project`, `person`, `done`, `q`, `team`), which `UI\TicketListContext` reads and checks for both pages; on the ticket page a filter the list would refuse only drops the context, and "N of M" with Previous and Next walk that list or, failing it, the plain open-tickets list. A ticket in the viewer's queue (`UI\TicketQueue`, kept in the session, at most 100 keys) shows the QueueBar instead of "N of M" and runs no position query, and its Back is a Stop that ends the queue and opens the list. The queue routes are in `UI\QueueController`, all POST with one CSRF id `ticketing_queue`: `ticketing_queue_start` (`/tickets/queue/start/list-step`, "Go through them" on a step of the list), `ticketing_queue_finish` (`/tickets/queue/finish`, Home with "All done in {label}.") and `ticketing_queue_stop` (`/t/{key}/queue/stop`, `back=1` opens the list). The Whose turn page is `GET /tickets/turn` (`ticketing_whose_turn`, `UI\WhoseTurnController`, `@Ticketing/whose_turn.html.twig`), read from Insight's `HomeQueries::whoseTurn(TicketViewer, ?string, ?string, ?TurnGroupCursor): WhoseTurnPage`: the viewer's card and 20 others per page, "Next page" going on by keyset with `?after=` (`UI\WhoseTurnCursorParameter`) and, on a later page, "Back to the start" going back to the first, `?open=` showing up to 100 tickets of one card, and each card's "Show N more" and "Show fewer" landing on its id; a malformed `after` or `open` is a 404. The page's People section (`UI\TicketPeople`, `Component\People`) is built from what the page already read, with no query of its own; its What happened section (`Component\Activity`, worded by `UI\TicketHistorySentences`) reads the latest 6 moves and checklist or verdict changes (the `ReviewChange` rows, P05.T7) through the Application port `TicketHistoryEntries::latestOf` (`Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalTicketHistoryEntries`), one query bounded by the ticket's `created_at`; `UI\TicketPageParts` builds both for the page and for a move's Turbo Stream. The demo seed `Infrastructure\Demo\SeedDemoTickets` (after `SeedDemoProjects`) makes 41 SHOP tickets, five in each core step plus one in Backlog with an ask past 700 characters that the ticket page's e2e spec opens, through `TicketCommands::create` and then one UPDATE that sets the step rather than `TicketCommands::move`, skipping the transition policy and the `moved` history row on purpose and setting directly the visibility, the reviewer in Code review, the tester in Testing and the label On hold waits on; a title SHOP already holds is kept, so a second run adds none. Tests make tickets with `tests/Factory/Ticketing/TicketFactory`.

The ticket page's Now and Next lines (`UI\NowNextSentences`) read the turn snapshot from `TicketQueries::pageOf` (`TicketPageView`), the saved turn and waiting turns from Insight's `TeamLoadQueries` and the sentences from Workflow's `TurnRules` and `NowNext`; `pageTurnOf` gives that read as a `UI\PageTurn`, which `linesOf` words and `UI\NextStepChoices` turns into the "Your next step" box's `UI\NextStep` (the sentence, Workflow's `TransitionPolicy::available` verbs and rule note, and the lead's `moveChoices` over `StepQueries::moveTargetsFor`).

`NextStep::peopleToWaitOn` lists, as `UI\PersonToWaitOn` (user id, first name, the roles joined by " · "), the ticket's named reporter, assignee, reviewer and tester once each who are still members of the organization (one `MembershipQueries::membersAmong` read), only when the lead's list offers a move to Needs input; the dialog ticks the first of them, or Someone else when none is left.

`NewTicketReport` is a port in Ticketing's Api that Review implements (`Review\UI\NewTicket\ScreenshotReportOnNewTicket`), so the new ticket form can check and save its screenshot part as Round 0 without Ticketing depending on Review: `refusalOf` before the ticket is made, `attach` right after it in the same transaction, which throws `NewTicketReportRefused` with the reason to show, so the caller rolls the ticket back. The posted shape is on the interface. `UI\NewTicketController` calls both inside its create transaction (P05.T5): `UI\TicketFormInput::fromRequest` reads the fields `screenshot_media_id`, `screenshot_title`, `screenshot_summary` and `pins[n][x|y|note]` raw into the port's shape (a missing one counts as blank, `pins` as none; any other value goes to the port as sent, so a list where text belongs is the port's plain refusal rather than a 400), `refusalOf` runs with the other field checks before `create`, and after it `TicketKeyQueries::idOf` finds the new ticket and `attach` saves the report; a `NewTicketReportRefused` from `attach` rolls the ticket and its number back. Either refusal answers 422 with the form as sent, its message as `errors.screenshot` and the posted report as `report` for `@Ticketing/new.html.twig`. `attach` fails with a `LogicException` when the request's tenant is not the organization it is given, before anything is read or saved, and throws `TicketNotFound` when a screenshot was posted and the tenant has no such ticket; neither can happen right after the form's own create in its own tenant, so both stay errors. `UI\Media\NewTicketMediaTarget` is the `MediaTargetAuthorizer` of kind `new_ticket`: the form's screenshot is uploaded before the ticket exists, so its target is a team in which `TicketPermission::CREATE` grants the actor new tickets. `TicketKeyQueries::idOf` gives the id of a ticket by its key, for the form to attach the report to the ticket it just made.

`ShareDraftQuestion` is a port in Ticketing's Api that Review implements (`Review\Application\RoundShareDraftQuestion`; `undecidedDraftOf` and `undecidedDraftIdOf` ask, `share(roundId, …)` and `keepPrivate(roundId, …)` answer), so a move asks "Share your draft round?" without Ticketing depending on Review (ADR-0017, hand-off; P05.T9). `TicketCommands::move` and `moveTo` take a last `?DraftChoice $draftChoice` (`Share` or `Keep`, default `Keep`) and share one path in `MoveTicket`: after every check of the move but the save, a move to another step reads the actor's own `UndecidedDraft` (open, unshared, not decided, with at least one screenshot; its id, screenshot count and the round number it would get); with a null choice it throws `DraftDecisionNeeded` carrying that draft and saves nothing, else it moves and then shares the draft or records it kept private in the same transaction, so a caller that does not ask, such as an API client, keeps it private. Nobody else's draft is touched. On the web both `POST /t/{key}/move/{verb}` and `POST /t/{key}/move-to/{stepId}` read the form field `draft_choice` (`share` or `keep`; absent or any other value counts as no choice) and answer a needed decision with 409 JSON `{draftDecisionNeeded: true, screenshotCount, roundNumber}`. `giveTo` never asks: the question belongs to moves that hand the ticket on to another step (P05.T9 step 1, ADR-0017), and a give only changes who has it (its one step change, Backlog to To do, is not a hand-off).

`ReviewChangeLabels` is a port in Ticketing's Api that Review implements (`Review\Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalReviewChangeLabels`): Review's events carry ids only (ADR-0027), so Ticketing asks it for a check item's pin number and a reviewed pin's number and area name (`ReviewedPin`), or an area's name, each null when the tenant has no such row. `Infrastructure\Messenger\RecordReviewChangesHandler` handles Review's four events `ChecklistItemChanged`, `PinVerdictSet`, `PinVerdictUndone` and `AreaVerdictSaved` in worker-notify, one typed method each, once per message under `ticketing.record_review_change`, and writes each as a `tkt_ticket_event` row (`TicketHistory::recordReviewChange`, types `check_item_changed`, `pin_verdict_set`, `pin_verdict_undone`, `area_verdict_saved`, payload `outcome`, `pin_number`, `area_name`, time to the microsecond); What happened reads them with the moves. Any other Review event has no handler here.

`NextStepBox` puts that move's chip in a dialog ("Who does it wait on?": one radio per person, "Someone else" with a one-line label, "Move"), which the `waiting-on` controller submits through the chip's own form; without JavaScript the chip posts directly and the ticket waits on its reporter.
