# Workflow

The steps, the five groups, the rules and the moves a ticket may make. It does not store tickets: Ticketing owns the current step and asks Workflow before every move. It owns the tables `wf_step` and `wf_rule_set`; the verb catalogue is data in code (`Domain\TransitionCatalogue`). Its Api is `StepQueries`, `RuleQueries`, `StepOccupancy`, `TransitionPolicy`, `TurnRules`, `NeedsYouScore`, `NowNext`, `VerbFor` and `WorkflowPermission`. It may use the Api of Organization. Every new organization gets the eight core steps and the default rule set in the transaction that creates it, through `Application\SetUpOrganizationWorkflow` (an `Organization\Api\OrganizationSetupStep`, [ADR-0036](../../docs/03-adr/0036-organization-setup-steps-in-the-creating-transaction.md)); organizations made before that got them from the migration `Version20261110140000`. Built so far of the Api: `StepQueries` (`stepsFor` a team in the groups' list order, then origin (core, company, team), then position, then id; `moveTargetsFor` a team, the same without the team's own steps under the step policy `locked`; `groupOf` a step, removed ones included), implemented with plain SQL by `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalStepQueries`; `RuleQueries` (`rulesFor` a team: its own rule set under the step policy `team` when it has one, else the organization's), implemented by `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalRuleQueries` through `DbalRuleSets`; `StepOccupancy` (`openTicketsIn` a step, asked by `Application\RemoveStep`), which Ticketing implements, as Workflow may not call Ticketing; `WorkflowPermission` (`STEP_CHANGE_TEAM`, `RULES_CHANGE_TEAM` on a `TeamId`), decided by `UI\Voter\WorkflowVoter` through `Application\StepChangeRights`: the team's Lead changes its steps under the step policy `extend` or `team` and its rules under `team`. Company steps, the organization's rules and the policy itself ask Organization's `ORG_ADMIN`. Built commands: `Application\AddStep`, `RemoveStep` and `SetRule`, for a company step or rule (no team) or a team's. `SetRule` writes only the switched rule's column (`DbalRuleSets::switchRule`: `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE SET` that one column, picked from a fixed map of the rule codes), so two people switching different rules at once keep both changes; a team row that does not exist yet starts as a copy of the organization's rules with that one rule switched. Events (`Api\Event\WorkflowEvent`: organization id and team id, both strings; no step names): `StepAdded` (step id, group code), `StepRemoved` (step id, removed at; not published for a step that was already removed) and `RulesChanged` (rule code, on or off; published on every set, also when the value did not change). The three commands publish them through `Application\WorkflowEvents` (`Infrastructure\Messenger\MessengerWorkflowEvents`, on `event.bus`) in the caller's transaction, which routes the interface to the outbox; the relay sends it to `async_notify`, where `Infrastructure\Messenger\InvalidateWorkflowCacheHandler` is the only consumer (it clears the team's cache). They carry no actor or time (except `removedAt`), as AddStep and SetRule take none. `stepsFor`, `moveTargetsFor` and `rulesFor` (a TeamId each) are cached per team for a day in the pool `cache.workflow` (`Infrastructure\Cache\{CachedStepQueries,CachedRuleQueries}` over `WorkflowCache`; the Dbal classes stay the source). Keys are `org.{o}.wf.team.{t}.{entry}` with entry `steps`, `move-targets.{policy}` or `rules.{policy}`, tagged `wf-team-{t}` and `wf-org-{o}`; the step policy is in the key, so a policy change needs no clear. A change queues its tags and `Infrastructure\Cache\WorkflowCacheTransactionMiddleware` (a DBAL middleware) clears them after the outermost commit; a company step or organization rule clears every team. `Infrastructure\Messenger\InvalidateWorkflowCacheHandler` clears again when the relayed event is handled. With no transaction or no tenant nothing is cached. `groupOf` is not cached. `TransitionPolicy` (`available` and `check`, each verb with its `MoveTarget` from `Domain\MoveSideEffects`; for the lead `moveChoices` and `checkMoveTo`, the "Move to another step…" list over the steps of `StepQueries::moveTargetsFor`, which both take; `checkMoveTo` refuses a step outside that list) is built by `Application\Transition\CatalogueTransitionPolicy` over the catalogue and the guards in `Application\Transition` (`TesterMovesDone`, `NoSelfReview`, `ReviewBeforeTest`); before them it runs the pause check `QuestionOpenPause`, once per ticket, and `MoveRights` says what a team role and job allow; it is pure: the caller passes the `TicketState`, an `ActorInTeam` (team role and job in the ticket's team), `RuleQueries::rulesFor` and the `TeamFacts` it gives `TurnRules::turnFor`, so the reviewer and tester it lets move a ticket are the ones the turn rules chose (`ReviewerChoice::reviewerOf`, `TesterChoice::testerOf`); in a Checking step the tester may also Move on, and `tester_moves_done` blocks nobody in a team without testers. `TurnRules::turnFor(TicketState, TeamFacts): Turn` is built by `Application\Turn\OrderedTurnRules` in the order of workflow-engine.md, "Whose turn", and is pure too: `TeamFacts` (lead id and display name, developers and testers as `TeamMemberLoad` with their open reviews and tickets in Testing) in, `Turn` (user id or label, `NeedsYouKind`, an override note for the caller to write to the ticket activity) out; without a reviewer or tester on the ticket `Application\Turn\{ReviewerChoice,TesterChoice}` choose. `NeedsYouScore::baseScore(NeedsYouKind, ?string $priority, ReleasePlacement, bool $isRiskCheck)` is implemented by `Application\Turn\WeightedNeedsYouScore`: kind base + priority weight + release points (`ReleasePlacement`: current, other or no release), +40 for a risk check; the time part is SQL at read time.

UI: `UI\TeamWorkflowController` serves the Team page's step and rule actions, `POST /teams/{slug}/steps` (`workflow_team_steps_add`), `/teams/{slug}/steps/remove` (`workflow_team_steps_remove`) and `/teams/{slug}/rules` (`workflow_team_rules_set`), each asking `WorkflowPermission` on the team and running in a transaction of its own through SharedKernel's port `Application\Transactions` (`Infrastructure\Doctrine\DoctrineTransactions`), the one Ticketing's project actions use.

See the [module catalogue](../../docs/02-architecture/modules.md#workflow-wf_). `NowNext::now` and `next` and `VerbFor::person` are pure too (`Application\Turn\{PlainNowNext,PlainVerbFor}`): the ticket, its `Turn` and a `NowNextContext` (step name, first names, `TeamFacts`, `review_before_test`, viewer, asker; without a stored reviewer or tester `next` names the one the team would pick) in, a sentence out; the viewer reads "you", a team step its name, and `next` is null for Done and team steps.
