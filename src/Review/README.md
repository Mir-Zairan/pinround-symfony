# Review

Review rounds with screenshots, pins and verdicts, including private draft rounds that are shared later. It owns the tables `rvw_round`, `rvw_screenshot`, `rvw_pin`, `rvw_check_item` and `rvw_area`.

## Depends on

- Ticketing Api: `TicketTurnQueries` (a ticket's reporter, to check who may add its report, and the ticket facts `RoundVoter` and `DraftRoundCreationVoter` read), `TicketListGroup` (Done), `TicketQueries`, `TicketVisibilities`, `TicketVisibility` (the viewer's, built by `RoundsController` and taken by `RoundsVisibleTo`) and `TicketVisibilityFacts` (what a person may see), `TicketPermission`, and `TicketNotFound` (`startDraft`'s, which `DraftRoundController` answers with the ticket page's 404). Who may add an upload to a round is its draft owner, or a person with VIEW and EDIT on the ticket. It implements Ticketing's port `NewTicketReport` with `UI\NewTicket\ScreenshotReportOnNewTicket` and throws its `NewTicketReportRefused`, and Ticketing's port `ShareDraftQuestion` with `Application\RoundShareDraftQuestion` (below), returning its `UndecidedDraft` and throwing its `TicketChangedMeanwhile`, and Ticketing's port `ReviewChangeLabels` with `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalReviewChangeLabels`, the pin numbers and area names of What happened, returning its `ReviewedPin`.
- Media Api: `MediaTargetAuthorizer`, which `UI\Media\RoundMediaTarget` (rounds), `UI\Media\NewReportMediaTarget` (`new_report`, a new report's project by its key) and `UI\Media\NotebookMediaTarget` (notebooks) implement, `SignedUrls`, which signs the pictures of the Rounds section, and `UploadedMedia`, whose `statesOf` `Application\ReportPictures` reads to tell `createReport` and the new ticket form whether a picture is the reporter's own and processed, and `Application\PicturesToKeep` reads to tell report drafts and notebooks whether a picture is the person's own and not rejected (still processing is fine).
- Organization Api: `CurrentActor`, the request's actor, and `MemberNameQueries`, the names of the rounds' owners.
- Identity Api: `CurrentUser`, the logged-in person `Application\Notebook\UseCaseNotebooks` acts for; and in the UI only `CurrentTimeZone` (the review round page's dates and the notebooks list's last use), `LoginAssurance` (`ReportDraftVoter` grants SEND in a browser session only) and `UserView` (a tester's first name in a checklist toast).

## What it has now

- Domain: `Round` (report and draft: adding, editing and removing a draft's pictures and pins, setting verdicts, sharing, deleting and deciding a draft, closing a shared round; every accepted change adds one to its version), `Screenshot`, `Pin`, their ids and enums (`DraftDecision`: `shared` or `kept_private`, what the owner chose at hand-off, asked once per draft), `RoundSlotTaken`, `RoundChanged` and `DraftHasNoScreenshots` (ADR-0048: `share` refuses a draft with no screenshot, "Add a screenshot before you share."). Tries (ADR-0048, P14.T6): `Round::addScreenshot` with `retakeOf` adds the next try of that screenshot's chain, stored in `rvw_screenshot.retake_of` as the chain's first try (same organization and round by a composite foreign key); it refuses a sixth try with `ScreenshotTriesFull` (`Screenshot::MAX_TRIES`, 5) and a screenshot not in the draft with `RetakenScreenshotNotFound`, and changes nothing. `removeScreenshot` of a first try makes the next try the first. `Round::setPinVerdict` marks a pin of an earlier try fixed or still wrong; fixed stores the chain's latest try number as it is then in `rvw_pin.fixed_on_try` (`Pin::fixedOnTry`), so a later retake leaves "Fixed on try N" as it was, and `removeScreenshot` clears a mark that named the removed try and counts one less on one that named a later try. A screenshot's number on its card is its chain's place among the round's chains, by first try, before and after share alike (`DbalRoundQueries` lists screenshots in that order, and `DbalChecklistQueries` labels a findings round's pins with the same number). `Round::tries()` gives each screenshot's `ScreenshotTry` ("Try n of m") by position, with no query. `DraftScreenshotCommands::addDraftScreenshotOnce` passes a retake from MCP `add_screenshot` and REST `POST /api/v1/tickets/{key}/draft/screenshots` (`retake_of`, below). `share` keeps every try with its pins; `Round::latestTries()` gives the last try of each chain by position, which is all the team sees of a shared round: `FindingsChecklist` makes items of its pins only, and `DbalRoundQueries::latestTrySql` filters `forTicket` (ticket page rows, cards and their counts, for the owner too) and `sharedRoundNotes` (MCP `get_round_notes`), an area's "before" in `DbalReviewRoundQueries::roundOf` and `DbalAreas::pinToCheckOf`. The owner alone reads the earlier tries, through `draftOf`, `Rounds::get` and `contentsOf`; a deleted draft is read by nobody, and every try goes with its round row (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). `Round::close` closes an open shared review round or shared draft and refuses Round 0, the checklist round, an unshared draft and a closed round; `isOpenSharedRound` and `isFindingsFor(assignee)` (a shared draft of anyone but the assignee) say which rounds the ADR-0048 rules count; `isClosedByMoveOf(mover, isMoversTurn, assignee)` says whether a move closes an open shared round (the mover's turn, or the assignee's move over their findings round, never a round the mover shared with "Share now"), `isClosedByShareOf(sharer, assignee)` whether the assignee's share closes an open findings round first, and `findingsReadyFor(assignee)` whom a round waits for when Review knows it (the assignee for their findings, else null). `UseCaseRoundCommands`' share and `RoundShareDraftQuestion` (below) read them under `openSharedRoundsOf`'s locks and close through `LockedRoundClosing::closeLockedRound`. `Rounds` is the repository port; `DbalRounds` implements it:
  - `get(RoundId)`: the tenant's round of any kind and state, a deleted draft too, with its screenshots (each with the first try it retakes) and pins.
  - `openUnsharedDraftOf(TicketId, UserId)`: the owner's open unshared draft on the ticket, at most one.
  - `openUnsharedDraftFactsOf(TicketId, UserId)`: the id, decision and screenshot count of that draft in one read (`DraftFacts`), without loading its screenshots and pins.
  - `nextFreeNumberOf(TicketId)`: one above the ticket's highest round number. It takes a transaction-scoped advisory lock on the ticket's round numbers (`pg_advisory_xact_lock` on `rvw_round_number:<organization>:<ticket>`), so a second caller waits until the first one's transaction ends. The unique index on the number stays as the backstop.
  - `openSharedRoundsOf(TicketId)`: the ticket's open shared rounds by number, with their screenshots and pins in three queries whatever their count. It takes the same number lock first, then `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on those rows (a verdict locks the row but takes no number lock), so of two "no open round, then share" requests the second waits and then sees the first one's share.
  - `save(Round, savedAt)`: writes the round's changes and its screenshots and pins (new ones added, changed ones updated, missing ones removed) only over the version the round was read at; if the stored version differs, it throws `RoundChanged` and writes nothing, so of two saves that each add a try to one chain from the same read the second is refused and a chain never passes 5 tries. A taken number throws `RoundSlotTaken`.
- Api: `RoundCommands`, `RoundQueries`, `RoundPermission` and `RoundRefused` (422, or 404 for `NOT_FOUND`). `RoundCommands` has `createReport` and ten draft commands, each in the caller's open transaction and each taking the time of the change: `startDraft`, `addDraftScreenshot`, `removeDraftScreenshot`, `editDraftScreenshot`, `setDraftPins`, `setDraftVerdict`, `setDraftVerdictWithSummary` (the verdict and, unless the summary is null, the summary in one save, with the stored title kept, for MCP `set_verdict`; the version goes up by one for the verdict and one for the summary, also when either is the one stored already, and a summary over 600 characters saves neither), `shareDraft`, `shareDraftAtHandOff` (shares and records `shared` in one save, for the move's dialog) and `deleteDraft`. A draft command works on the owner's own draft only; an unknown or malformed id, another organization's round, another person's draft, a round that is not a draft and a deleted draft all answer `NOT_FOUND`. `createReport` refuses (422) content the domain refuses, someone who is not the reporter, a picture the reporter did not upload or that is still processing or rejected (its `MEDIA_` messages; checked before anything is built), or a ticket that already has a report. The draft commands add these reasons: `NOT_FOUND`, `DRAFT_EXISTS` (the owner has an open draft on the ticket), `DRAFT_CHANGED` (another request saved the draft first), `SCREENSHOT_NOT_FOUND`, `UNKNOWN_VERDICT` and `NUMBER_TAKEN` (a round saved without `RoundCommands` took the number during a share). A caller that answers by kind rather than by text asks `RoundRefused`: `isNotFound` (`NOT_FOUND` or `SCREENSHOT_NOT_FOUND`), `isReadOnly` (the domain refused a change of a draft that was shared or closed before it; made by `RoundRefused::readOnly`) and `isInvalidContent` (a text over its cap, a pin outside the picture, too many pictures or pins, `UNKNOWN_VERDICT`; made by `RoundRefused::invalidContent`); `DRAFT_CHANGED`, `DRAFT_EXISTS`, `NUMBER_TAKEN`, a `MEDIA_` message and a decision made already are of none of these kinds. `RoundCommands` also holds the caps a caller's own form or schema shows: `MAX_PINS_PER_SCREENSHOT`, `MAX_TITLE_LENGTH`, `MAX_SUMMARY_LENGTH` and `MAX_PIN_NOTE_LENGTH`.
- Api: `DraftScreenshotCommands::addDraftScreenshotOnce`, for MCP `add_screenshot`, adds an uploaded picture with its title, summary and pins to the owner's draft in one read and one save, or, when the draft holds that media id already (in any case), answers that screenshot with `wasAddedBefore` and saves nothing (`AddedDraftScreenshot`). With `retakeOf`, any try of a screenshot of that draft, the picture is the next try of its chain (`Round::addScreenshot`); a `retakeOf` that is malformed or no screenshot of that draft (another person's draft, a shared round, another organization) is `SCREENSHOT_NOT_FOUND`, not found at the MCP and REST edge, checked after the picture, and a sixth try is the plain refusal `ScreenshotTriesFull::REASON` (REST 422, outcome `refused`). It refuses as the draft commands do; a shared or closed draft is read-only also when it holds the picture. `UPLOAD_TARGET_KIND` is the kind of Media's upload target for a round (`RoundMediaTarget::KIND`). `UseCaseRoundCommands` implements it.
- Api, reads: `RoundQueries` has six methods, all in the caller's open transaction under the tenant. `draftScreenshotOf(ticket, screenshotId)` returns the `RoundAccessFacts` of the ticket's draft that holds the picture, in one query whatever the number of rounds (a shared draft past the newest 20 is found too), with the id in any case; another person's draft, shared or not, and a deleted one come back as they are for `RoundVoter` to deny; null for a malformed id (without a query) and a picture of a report, of another ticket, of another organization or unknown. `sharedRoundNotes(ticket)` returns the newest shared report or draft round with its pictures (the latest try of each) and the pins that count, in one query, and checks nobody. `forTicket` returns `TicketRounds` (a deleted draft is hidden). `accessFactsOf(roundId)` returns `RoundAccessFacts` (organization, ticket, owner, whether it is an unshared draft, whether it is deleted) in one query for `RoundVoter`, or null for a malformed id, an unknown id and another organization's round (row-level security hides it), and takes no visibility decision itself. `roundNotes(ticket, owner)` returns `RoundNotes`, the owner's open unshared draft of the ticket with its `ScreenshotNotes` by position, each with its `PinNote`s (number, x and y in percent, note) by number, only the pins that count (a person's, and an AI pin a person kept), in one query, or null when the owner has no such draft; another person's draft, a shared round, a deleted draft and another ticket's draft are never in it. `draftOf(roundId, owner)` returns the owner's own undeleted draft as a `RoundView` with all its screenshots and pins (three queries at most, none of any other round), or null for another person's draft, a round that is no draft, a deleted draft, another organization's round and an unknown or malformed id; `DraftRoundEditor` re-renders from it after each live action, so its card shows every screenshot of an unshared draft, up to the domain's 50, while other cards keep their 10 pictures. `OpenDraftQueries::openDraftOf(ticket, owner)` returns only the `RoundAccessFacts` of the owner's open unshared draft of the ticket, in one query on `rvw_round_ticket_id_owner_id_open_draft_key` without its pictures or pins, or null; another person's draft, a shared, closed or deleted one and another organization's round never come back. It checks neither who asks nor the ticket's visibility (MCP `create_upload` asks `TicketPermission::VIEW` itself); `DbalRoundQueries` implements it too.
- Api, permissions: `RoundPermission` has `VIEW`, `EDIT` (subject `RoundAccessFacts`) and `DRAFT_CREATE` (subject `TicketId`). Who may do what, as the [roles matrix](../../docs/00-product/personas-and-roles.md) rows "See a draft round" and "Start / edit / share a draft round" have it: an unshared draft is its owner's alone to view and edit, and not a lead's or an admin's either; a shared round is viewed by whoever may view its ticket (`TicketPermission::VIEW`) and edited by whoever may view and edit it (`TicketPermission::EDIT`); a deleted draft is nobody's; `DRAFT_CREATE` is the ticket's current assignee while the ticket is not in Done. A caller answers a denied `VIEW` or `EDIT` with 404, never 403, the same as an unknown round id, so a draft's existence does not leak.
- Application: `UseCaseRoundCommands`, `ReportPictures` (the one rule, for `createReport` and the new ticket form, that a report's pictures are the reporter's own and processed, answered as a `RoundRefused` `MEDIA_` message), `PicturesToKeep` (the one rule, for report drafts and notebooks, that a kept picture is the person's own and not rejected, still processing allowed, answered the same way), `RoundsVisibleTo` (`RoundQueries` for a caller that has built the viewer's `TicketVisibility`; `DbalRoundQueries` implements both), and `RoundUploadTargets`, the read port `UI\Media\RoundMediaTarget` uses to read a round as an upload target; `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalRoundUploadTargets` implements it. Media never calls it. `RoundShareDraftQuestion` answers Ticketing's "Share your draft round?" when a verb move or a move to a chosen step hands the ticket on (ADR-0017, hand-off; P05.T9), in the move's transaction and on the mover's own draft only: `undecidedDraftOf(ticket, MoveFacts)` gives the mover's open unshared draft that has no decision and at least one screenshot, with the number it would get, and the lowest-numbered open shared round that neither this move nor the mover's share would close, which blocks sharing it (`openRoundNumber`, and `openRoundReadyForUserId`, the assignee for their findings round), taking the round number lock and the open rounds' row locks, or null; `undecidedDraftIdOf` gives only its id, from `Rounds::openUnsharedDraftFactsOf`, with no number lock; `closeRoundsOnMove(ticket, MoveFacts, at)` closes the open shared rounds the move ends (ADR-0048): none for a lead-only move or a move by someone whose turn it was not and who is not the assignee, which read no round; else each `Round::isClosedByMoveOf` closes; `share(roundId, …)` shares it through `RoundCommands::shareDraftAtHandOff` and records `shared` in one save, after the move's close, so a closing hand-off shares as the next number; a share still blocked by an open round is Ticketing's `MoveRefused` with the round's reason; `keepPrivate(roundId, …)` records `kept_private`. A draft changed by another request meanwhile is Ticketing's `TicketChangedMeanwhile`, and the move rolls back. A move that both closes and shares reads and locks the open shared rounds twice, once to close and once in the share's check.
- Api: `ReportDrafts` (ADR-0039, ADR-0040), the owner-only report drafts in `rvw_report_draft`: `create`, `forOwner`, `ofOwner`, `update`, `addScreenshot`, `delete` and `send`, which makes the ticket through Ticketing's `TicketCommands::report` (Bug, Normal, the project's team, and the person picked in "Give it to" or none; the draft page shows Ticketing's `ReportGiveToQueries` choice, the same as Report a problem's, and `send` refuses a person it does not offer with `ReportGiveTo::NOT_OFFERED` (P14.T9); Ticketing works out the check reasons and team-only visibility) and Round 0 through `createReport`, then removes the draft, in the caller's transaction. `ReportDraftRefused` carries the plain message; `NOT_FOUND` is a 404. `ReportDraftPermission` VIEW, EDIT and SEND; `Application\ReportDraft\ReportDraftContentRules` holds the caps. `create_report` (MCP) is the only maker today; `create_report_upload` begins its pictures on the upload target kind `new_report` (`UI\Media\NewReportMediaTarget`, a project a team owns).
- UI: `ReportDraftController`, "Report drafts" at `/report-drafts`: the owner's list, one draft's page (edit the title, detail, each picture's title, summary and pin notes, remove a picture), Save, Send and Delete; anyone else gets the one 404. `Voter\ReportDraftVoter` grants the owner only, and SEND only in a browser session, never to a personal access token.
- UI: `NotebookController`, "My notebooks" (P13.T6, ADR-0046): the list at `/notebooks` (last used first, keyset-paged by 20 with "Show more"; a malformed cursor is a 404), one notebook's page at `/notebooks/{id}` (its pictures with their pins, and its scenarios by title; reading it is no use), and Rename (`/notebooks/{id}/rename`) and Delete (`/notebooks/{id}/delete`), each in its own transaction with a CSRF token per notebook. Owner only: another person's notebook, another organization's, an unknown or malformed id, and one another tab deleted meanwhile are the one 404 `NotebookRefused::NOT_FOUND`, before the form is read; an expired form or `NOTEBOOK_CHANGED` is a toast and changes nothing, and a refused rename shows the rule's words over the form. The notebook page and the report draft page draw their pictures and pins through the shared `ScreenshotWithPinsViews`; the report drafts list links to "My notebooks".
- UI: `Voter\RoundVoter` decides `VIEW` and `EDIT` on a `RoundAccessFacts` and `Voter\DraftRoundCreationVoter` decides `DRAFT_CREATE` on a `TicketId` (apart because a voter decides one subject class); `VoterMatrixTest` has their rows. The Rounds section of the ticket page (`Component\RoundsSection`, a heading and a lazy Turbo frame, embedded by name; `RoundsController` at `/t/{key}/rounds` fills the frame with the cards `RoundCards` builds: Round 0, the newest 20 numbered rounds and the viewer's own draft, at most 10 pictures a card), `DraftRoundController`, the private draft round's buttons (`review_draft_start`, `review_draft_share`, `review_draft_delete`, each in a transaction of its own with a CSRF token per ticket key: "Start a draft round" shows only when `DRAFT_CREATE` grants it and the viewer's own unshared draft is not on the page; a ticket the person may not see is the ticket page's 404, and a denied start on a ticket they may see is a toast, "Only the assignee, or the person whose turn it is, can start a draft round.", and changes nothing; `DRAFT_CREATE` grants the assignee and the person whose turn it is in Insight's turn read model (ADR-0044); share and delete answer an unknown or malformed id, a round of another ticket, and a denied `VIEW` or `EDIT` with the one 404 `RoundRefused::NOT_FOUND`; any other refusal, and an expired form, is a toast and changes nothing), the owner's draft card with its PRIVATE tag, "Share with the team now", "Delete draft" (the browser asks first, through `data-turbo-confirm`) and the "For Claude Code" box, the live component `Component\DraftRoundEditor` inside that card (live actions `addScreenshot`, `removeScreenshot`, `editScreenshotText`, `setVerdict` and `setPins`, each saved through the draft commands; every live request first finds the ticket for the person and asks `VIEW` and `EDIT` on the round, and anyone else gets `RoundRefused::NOT_FOUND` as a 404; a refusal of a command is shown in the card and saves nothing; a pin that is not `{x, y, note}` is a 400; `DraftRoundEditorTest` proves the actions and who can reach them), the screenshot and pin components, and `RoundMediaTarget`, its `MediaTargetAuthorizer` for kind `round`: Media asks it who may add an upload to a round. `NewTicket\ScreenshotReportOnNewTicket` reads the new ticket form's posted screenshot fields (a shape it cannot read gets a plain message, never another exception; pins in the order of their posted number), checks the picture with `ReportPictures` and saves it through `createReport`; it fails with a `LogicException` when the request's tenant is not the organization it is given. `Component\ScreenshotReportField` is the form's Screenshot section: it posts `screenshot_media_id`, `screenshot_title`, `screenshot_summary` and `pins[n][x|y|note]`, and re-rendered after a refusal it keeps the title and summary, and the picture with its pins only when the picture is the logged-in person's own in their organization and not rejected (its row in [frontend.md](../../docs/02-architecture/frontend.md#design-system-twig-components); its browser side is the `screenshot-report` controller under [Stimulus controllers](../../docs/02-architecture/frontend.md#stimulus-controllers)).

- Checklist (P05.T6): `ChecklistCommands` (`createFromReport`, `markFixed`, `markOk`, `markNotFixed`; `Application\Checklist\TodoStep` is the one definition of the To do rule) and `ChecklistQueries` (`forTicket`, `accessFactsOf`) in the Api, with `ChecklistPermission` (`MARK_FIXED`, `CHECK`) that `UI\Voter\CheckItemVoter` decides on a `CheckItemAccessFacts`: the assignee marks fixed and the tester checks, each needing VIEW and EDIT on the ticket. `Domain\Checklists` is the port `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalChecklists` implements: one open `rvw_check_item` per pin of Round 0, written with its checklist round in one multi-row insert, which the caps (50 screenshots, 100 pins, 8 columns) keep under PostgreSQL's 65,535 bound values (`DbalChecklistsBoundValuesTest`); a ticket has one checklist round (partial unique index), and a check item goes with its pin or round (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). `Infrastructure\Messenger\CreateChecklistOnTicketEventsHandler` makes the checklist when Ticketing's `TicketCreated` or `TicketMoved` arrives for a ticket in To do, or on the verb `start_work`; `createReport` makes it itself when the ticket is already in To do and refuses to make one when the report is refused.

- Review rounds (P05.T7, P05.T10): `rvw_area` holds a review round's screen areas, each comparing this round's "after" picture with the earlier round's "before". `ReviewRoundQueries::roundOf` reads one shared round for a viewer who may see its ticket (only pins that count: a person's, or an AI pin a person kept). `VerdictCommands` (`setPinVerdict`, `undoPinVerdict`, `saveAreaVerdict`, `Application\Verdict\UseCaseVerdictCommands` over the port `Domain\Areas`, which `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalAreas` implements; the round row is locked first) refuses with `VerdictRefused` and publishes `PinVerdictSet`, `PinVerdictUndone` or `AreaVerdictSaved` (`Api\Event`, through `Infrastructure\Messenger\MessengerReviewEvents`). `VerdictPermission::SET` (`UI\Voter\VerdictVoter`): the ticket's tester and the team's lead. UI: the round page `GET /t/{key}/rounds/{number}` and the full-screen page `…/focus` (`UI\Controller\RoundController`, `UI\RoundPage\*`), the verdict POSTs (`UI\Controller\VerdictController`), and the checklist cards in the Rounds section, the checklist round's and each findings round's under its pictures, read for all rounds in one query through `Application\Checklist\RoundChecklistQueries` (`UI\Checklist\*`, `UI\Controller\ChecklistController`). No verdict moves the ticket: once every area has one, the tester is offered the matching verb (see [workflow-engine.md](../../docs/02-architecture/workflow-engine.md#transition-catalogue)). Nothing in the product makes a review round yet; the dev/test-only `Infrastructure\Console\SeedReviewRoundCommand` (`app:e2e:seed-review-round`) writes one for the browser tests.

- Notebooks (P13.T6, ADR-0046), storage, Api, voter and page (below); the MCP tools `list_my_notebooks`, `get_notebook`, `save_scenarios`, `create_notebook`, `create_notebook_upload` and `add_notebook_screenshot` (AgentApi) call it, the last three also through REST `/api/v1/notebooks`: `Domain\Notebook\Notebook` is a person's private notebook (title, up to 10 screenshots with their pins, and the `Domain\Scenario\Scenarios` that `ScenarioRules` accepted, which hold the one cap of 20), refusing past its caps, text with a NUL character or invalid UTF-8, and a new title with a C0 control character or DEL inside it (around it they are trimmed; a stored title is read back as it is), with a plain `NotebookRefused` message; `NotebookScreenshot` and `NotebookPin` keep a round screenshot's and pin's caps. `Domain\Notebook\NotebookRows` is the port `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalNotebooks` implements on `rvw_notebook`: `insert`, `ofOwner` (scenarios read back through `ScenarioRules::parse`), `forOwner` (last used first, keyset, 1 to 100 a page), `save` (only over the version it read), `markUsed` (last use only, version kept) and `delete`, each naming the owner. Api: `Notebooks` (`create`, `addScreenshot`, which answers `AddedNotebookScreenshot` and saves nothing for a picture the notebook holds already, `rename`, `delete`, `forOwner`, `get`, `replaceScenarios`; the caps `MAX_SCREENSHOTS`, `MAX_SCENARIOS` and `MAX_TITLE_LENGTH`, 120, which `NotebookCapsAgreeTest` holds equal to the domain's, the table's and `docs/api/v1.yaml`'s), `Application\Notebook\UseCaseNotebooks`, for the request's logged-in person only; `get` asks `NotebookPermission::VIEW` and each write `EDIT` (`create` before the notebook exists; `NotebookView::notMadeYet` is that notebook for a caller that asks the voter itself first, as `create_notebook`'s gate does) through the port `NotebookAccess` (`UI\Voter\SymfonyNotebookAccess`, decided by `UI\Voter\NotebookVoter`, the owner alone), and a write saves over the version it read; `forOwner` asks no voter, as it lists only that person's rows of the tenant. Anyone else's notebook, another organization's, a malformed or unknown id, a voter denial and nobody logged in are `NotebookRefused::NOT_FOUND` (`get` gives null); with nobody logged in `create` and `forOwner` throw a `LogicException`; a stale version is `NOTEBOOK_CHANGED`; nothing is saved on a refusal. Pictures upload on the target kind `notebook` (`UI\Media\NotebookMediaTarget`); `Application\PicturesToKeep` takes a picture still processing and refuses a rejected one or one not the person's upload in the tenant, as for report drafts. Proven by `UseCaseNotebooksTest`, `NotebookLeakTest`, `NotebookCapsAgreeTest` and `NotebookControllerTest`.

Not built yet: a command that makes a review round, the ticket event on share, and AI pins (P08). See the phase 5 hand-off notes for what P05.T8 still lacks.
