# Review

Review rounds with screenshots, pins and verdicts, including private draft rounds that are shared later. It owns the tables `rvw_round`, `rvw_screenshot`, `rvw_pin` and `rvw_check_item`.

## Depends on

- Ticketing Api: `TicketTurnQueries` (a ticket's reporter, to check who may add its report, and the ticket facts `RoundVoter` and `DraftRoundCreationVoter` read), `TicketListGroup` (Done), `TicketQueries`, `TicketVisibilities`, `TicketVisibility` (the viewer's, built by `RoundsController` and taken by `RoundsVisibleTo`) and `TicketVisibilityFacts` (what a person may see), `TicketPermission`, and `TicketNotFound` (`startDraft`'s, which `DraftRoundController` answers with the ticket page's 404). Who may add an upload to a round is its draft owner, or a person with VIEW and EDIT on the ticket. It implements Ticketing's port `NewTicketReport` with `UI\NewTicket\ScreenshotReportOnNewTicket` and throws its `NewTicketReportRefused`, and Ticketing's port `ShareDraftQuestion` with `Application\RoundShareDraftQuestion` (below), returning its `UndecidedDraft` and throwing its `TicketChangedMeanwhile`.
- Media Api: `MediaTargetAuthorizer`, which `UI\Media\RoundMediaTarget` implements, `SignedUrls`, which signs the pictures of the Rounds section, and `UploadedMedia`, whose `statesOf` `Application\ReportPictures` reads to tell `createReport` and the new ticket form whether a picture is the reporter's own and processed.
- Organization Api: `CurrentActor`, the request's actor, and `MemberNameQueries`, the names of the rounds' owners.

## What it has now

- Domain: `Round` (report and draft: adding, editing and removing a draft's pictures and pins, setting verdicts, sharing, deleting and deciding a draft; every accepted change adds one to its version), `Screenshot`, `Pin`, their ids and enums (`DraftDecision`: `shared` or `kept_private`, what the owner chose at hand-off, asked once per draft), `RoundSlotTaken` and `RoundChanged`. `Rounds` is the repository port; `DbalRounds` implements it:
  - `get(RoundId)`: the tenant's round of any kind and state, a deleted draft too, with its screenshots and pins.
  - `openUnsharedDraftOf(TicketId, UserId)`: the owner's open unshared draft on the ticket, at most one.
  - `nextFreeNumberOf(TicketId)`: one above the ticket's highest round number. It takes a transaction-scoped advisory lock on the ticket's round numbers (`pg_advisory_xact_lock` on `rvw_round_number:<organization>:<ticket>`), so a second caller waits until the first one's transaction ends. The unique index on the number stays as the backstop.
  - `save(Round, savedAt)`: writes the round's changes and its screenshots and pins (new ones added, changed ones updated, missing ones removed) only over the version the round was read at; if the stored version differs, it throws `RoundChanged` and writes nothing. A taken number throws `RoundSlotTaken`.
- Api: `RoundCommands`, `RoundQueries`, `RoundPermission` and `RoundRefused` (422, or 404 for `NOT_FOUND`). `RoundCommands` has `createReport` and eight draft commands, each in the caller's open transaction and each taking the time of the change: `startDraft`, `addDraftScreenshot`, `removeDraftScreenshot`, `editDraftScreenshot`, `setDraftPins`, `setDraftVerdict`, `shareDraft` and `deleteDraft`. A draft command works on the owner's own draft only; an unknown or malformed id, another organization's round, another person's draft, a round that is not a draft and a deleted draft all answer `NOT_FOUND`. `createReport` refuses (422) content the domain refuses, someone who is not the reporter, a picture the reporter did not upload or that is still processing or rejected (its `MEDIA_` messages; checked before anything is built), or a ticket that already has a report. The draft commands add these reasons: `NOT_FOUND`, `DRAFT_EXISTS` (the owner has an open draft on the ticket), `DRAFT_CHANGED` (another request saved the draft first), `SCREENSHOT_NOT_FOUND`, `UNKNOWN_VERDICT` and `NUMBER_TAKEN` (a round saved without `RoundCommands` took the number during a share).
- Api, reads: `RoundQueries` has four methods, all in the caller's open transaction under the tenant. `forTicket` returns `TicketRounds` (a deleted draft is hidden). `accessFactsOf(roundId)` returns `RoundAccessFacts` (organization, ticket, owner, whether it is an unshared draft, whether it is deleted) in one query for `RoundVoter`, or null for a malformed id, an unknown id and another organization's round (row-level security hides it), and takes no visibility decision itself. `roundNotes(ticket, owner)` returns `RoundNotes`, the owner's open unshared draft of the ticket with its `ScreenshotNotes` by position, each with its `PinNote`s (number, x and y in percent, note) by number, in one query, or null when the owner has no such draft; another person's draft, a shared round, a deleted draft and another ticket's draft are never in it. `draftOf(roundId, owner)` returns the owner's own undeleted draft as a `RoundView` with all its screenshots and pins (three queries at most, none of any other round), or null for another person's draft, a round that is no draft, a deleted draft, another organization's round and an unknown or malformed id; `DraftRoundEditor` re-renders from it after each live action, so its card shows every screenshot of an unshared draft, up to the domain's 50, while other cards keep their 10 pictures.
- Api, permissions: `RoundPermission` has `VIEW`, `EDIT` (subject `RoundAccessFacts`) and `DRAFT_CREATE` (subject `TicketId`). Who may do what, as the [roles matrix](../../docs/00-product/personas-and-roles.md) rows "See a draft round" and "Start / edit / share a draft round" have it: an unshared draft is its owner's alone to view and edit, and not a lead's or an admin's either; a shared round is viewed by whoever may view its ticket (`TicketPermission::VIEW`) and edited by whoever may view and edit it (`TicketPermission::EDIT`); a deleted draft is nobody's; `DRAFT_CREATE` is the ticket's current assignee while the ticket is not in Done. A caller answers a denied `VIEW` or `EDIT` with 404, never 403, the same as an unknown round id, so a draft's existence does not leak.
- Application: `UseCaseRoundCommands`, `ReportPictures` (the one rule, for `createReport` and the new ticket form, that a report's pictures are the reporter's own and processed, answered as a `RoundRefused` `MEDIA_` message), `RoundsVisibleTo` (`RoundQueries` for a caller that has built the viewer's `TicketVisibility`; `DbalRoundQueries` implements both), and `RoundUploadTargets`, the read port `UI\Media\RoundMediaTarget` uses to read a round as an upload target; `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalRoundUploadTargets` implements it. Media never calls it. `RoundShareDraftQuestion` answers Ticketing's "Share your draft round?" when a verb move or a move to a chosen step hands the ticket on (ADR-0017, hand-off; P05.T9), in the move's transaction and on the mover's own draft only: `undecidedDraftOf` gives the owner's open unshared draft that has no decision and at least one screenshot, with the number it would get (taking the round number lock), or null; `share` shares it through `RoundCommands::shareDraft` and records `shared`; `keepPrivate` records `kept_private`. A draft changed by another request meanwhile is Ticketing's `TicketChangedMeanwhile`, and the move rolls back.
- UI: `Voter\RoundVoter` decides `VIEW` and `EDIT` on a `RoundAccessFacts` and `Voter\DraftRoundCreationVoter` decides `DRAFT_CREATE` on a `TicketId` (apart because a voter decides one subject class); `VoterMatrixTest` has their rows. The Rounds section of the ticket page (`Component\RoundsSection`, a heading and a lazy Turbo frame, embedded by name; `RoundsController` at `/t/{key}/rounds` fills the frame with the cards `RoundCards` builds: Round 0, the newest 20 numbered rounds and the viewer's own draft, at most 10 pictures a card), `DraftRoundController`, the private draft round's buttons (`review_draft_start`, `review_draft_share`, `review_draft_delete`, each in a transaction of its own with a CSRF token per ticket key: "Start a draft round" shows only when `DRAFT_CREATE` grants it and the viewer's own unshared draft is not on the page; a ticket the person may not see is the ticket page's 404, and a denied start on a ticket they may see is a toast, "Only the ticket's assignee can start a draft round.", and changes nothing; share and delete answer an unknown or malformed id, a round of another ticket, and a denied `VIEW` or `EDIT` with the one 404 `RoundRefused::NOT_FOUND`; any other refusal, and an expired form, is a toast and changes nothing), the owner's draft card with its PRIVATE tag, "Share with the team now", "Delete draft" (the browser asks first, through `data-turbo-confirm`) and the "For Claude Code" box, the live component `Component\DraftRoundEditor` inside that card (live actions `addScreenshot`, `removeScreenshot`, `editScreenshotText`, `setVerdict` and `setPins`, each saved through the draft commands; every live request first finds the ticket for the person and asks `VIEW` and `EDIT` on the round, and anyone else gets `RoundRefused::NOT_FOUND` as a 404; a refusal of a command is shown in the card and saves nothing; a pin that is not `{x, y, note}` is a 400; `DraftRoundEditorTest` proves the actions and who can reach them), the screenshot and pin components, and `RoundMediaTarget`, its `MediaTargetAuthorizer` for kind `round`: Media asks it who may add an upload to a round. `NewTicket\ScreenshotReportOnNewTicket` reads the new ticket form's posted screenshot fields (a shape it cannot read gets a plain message, never another exception; pins in the order of their posted number), checks the picture with `ReportPictures` and saves it through `createReport`; it fails with a `LogicException` when the request's tenant is not the organization it is given. `Component\ScreenshotReportField` is the form's Screenshot section: it posts `screenshot_media_id`, `screenshot_title`, `screenshot_summary` and `pins[n][x|y|note]`, and re-rendered after a refusal it keeps the title and summary, and the picture with its pins only when the picture is the logged-in person's own in their organization and not rejected (its row in [frontend.md](../../docs/02-architecture/frontend.md#design-system-twig-components); its browser side is the `screenshot-report` controller under [Stimulus controllers](../../docs/02-architecture/frontend.md#stimulus-controllers)).

- Checklist (P05.T6): `ChecklistCommands` (`createFromReport`, `markFixed`, `markOk`, `markNotFixed`; `Application\Checklist\TodoStep` is the one definition of the To do rule) and `ChecklistQueries` (`forTicket`, `accessFactsOf`) in the Api, with `ChecklistPermission` (`MARK_FIXED`, `CHECK`) that `UI\Voter\CheckItemVoter` decides on a `CheckItemAccessFacts`: the assignee marks fixed and the tester checks, each needing VIEW and EDIT on the ticket. `Domain\Checklists` is the port `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalChecklists` implements: one open `rvw_check_item` per pin of Round 0, written with its checklist round in one multi-row insert, which the caps (50 screenshots, 100 pins, 8 columns) keep under PostgreSQL's 65,535 bound values (`DbalChecklistsBoundValuesTest`); a ticket has one checklist round (partial unique index), and a check item goes with its pin or round (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). `Infrastructure\Messenger\CreateChecklistOnTicketEventsHandler` makes the checklist when Ticketing's `TicketCreated` or `TicketMoved` arrives for a ticket in To do, or on the verb `start_work`; `createReport` makes it itself when the ticket is already in To do and refuses to make one when the report is refused.

Not built yet: review rounds, the ticket event on share, AI pins and the checklist card. See the phase 5 hand-off notes for what P05.T8 still lacks.
