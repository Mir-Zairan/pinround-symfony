# Review

Review rounds with screenshots, pins and verdicts, including private draft rounds that are shared later. It owns the tables `rvw_round`, `rvw_screenshot`, `rvw_pin` and `rvw_check_item`.

## Depends on

- Ticketing Api: `TicketTurnQueries` (a ticket's reporter, to check who may add its report), `TicketQueries`, `TicketVisibilities`, `TicketVisibility` (the viewer's, built by `RoundsController` and taken by `RoundsVisibleTo`) and `TicketVisibilityFacts` (what a person may see), and `TicketPermission`. Who may add an upload to a round is its draft owner, or a person with VIEW and EDIT on the ticket. It implements Ticketing's port `NewTicketReport` with `UI\NewTicket\ScreenshotReportOnNewTicket` and throws its `NewTicketReportRefused`.
- Media Api: `MediaTargetAuthorizer`, which `UI\Media\RoundMediaTarget` implements, `SignedUrls`, which signs the pictures of the Rounds section, and `UploadedMedia`, whose `statesOf` `Application\ReportPictures` reads to tell `createReport` and the new ticket form whether a picture is the reporter's own and processed.
- Organization Api: `CurrentActor`, the request's actor, and `MemberNameQueries`, the names of the rounds' owners.

## What it has now

- Domain: `Round` (report and draft: adding, editing and removing a draft's pictures and pins, setting verdicts, sharing, deleting and deciding a draft; every accepted change adds one to its version), `Screenshot`, `Pin`, their ids and enums (`DraftDecision`: `shared` or `kept_private`, what the owner chose at hand-off, asked once per draft), `RoundSlotTaken` and `RoundChanged`. `Rounds` is the repository port; `DbalRounds` implements it:
  - `get(RoundId)`: the tenant's round of any kind and state, a deleted draft too, with its screenshots and pins.
  - `openUnsharedDraftOf(TicketId, UserId)`: the owner's open unshared draft on the ticket, at most one.
  - `nextFreeNumberOf(TicketId)`: one above the ticket's highest round number. It takes a transaction-scoped advisory lock on the ticket's round numbers (`pg_advisory_xact_lock` on `rvw_round_number:<organization>:<ticket>`), so a second caller waits until the first one's transaction ends. The unique index on the number stays as the backstop.
  - `save(Round, savedAt)`: writes the round's changes and its screenshots and pins (new ones added, changed ones updated, missing ones removed) only over the version the round was read at; if the stored version differs, it throws `RoundChanged` and writes nothing. A taken number throws `RoundSlotTaken`.
- Api: `RoundCommands`, `RoundQueries` (`forTicket` returns `TicketRounds`; a deleted draft is hidden), and `RoundRefused` (422, or 404 for `NOT_FOUND`). `RoundCommands` has `createReport` and eight draft commands, each in the caller's open transaction and each taking the time of the change: `startDraft`, `addDraftScreenshot`, `removeDraftScreenshot`, `editDraftScreenshot`, `setDraftPins`, `setDraftVerdict`, `shareDraft` and `deleteDraft`. A draft command works on the owner's own draft only; an unknown or malformed id, another organization's round, another person's draft, a round that is not a draft and a deleted draft all answer `NOT_FOUND`. `createReport` refuses (422) content the domain refuses, someone who is not the reporter, a picture the reporter did not upload or that is still processing or rejected (its `MEDIA_` messages; checked before anything is built), or a ticket that already has a report. The draft commands add these reasons: `NOT_FOUND`, `DRAFT_EXISTS` (the owner has an open draft on the ticket), `DRAFT_CHANGED` (another request saved the draft first), `SCREENSHOT_NOT_FOUND`, `UNKNOWN_VERDICT` and `NUMBER_TAKEN` (a round saved without `RoundCommands` took the number during a share).
- Application: `UseCaseRoundCommands`, `ReportPictures` (the one rule, for `createReport` and the new ticket form, that a report's pictures are the reporter's own and processed, answered as a `RoundRefused` `MEDIA_` message), `RoundsVisibleTo` (`RoundQueries` for a caller that has built the viewer's `TicketVisibility`; `DbalRoundQueries` implements both), and `RoundUploadTargets`, the read port `UI\Media\RoundMediaTarget` uses to read a round as an upload target; `Infrastructure\Doctrine\DbalRoundUploadTargets` implements it. Media never calls it.
- UI: the Rounds section of the ticket page (`Component\RoundsSection`, a heading and a lazy Turbo frame, embedded by name; `RoundsController` at `/t/{key}/rounds` fills the frame with the cards `RoundCards` builds: Round 0, the newest 20 numbered rounds and the viewer's own draft, at most 10 pictures a card), the screenshot and pin components, and `RoundMediaTarget`, its `MediaTargetAuthorizer` for kind `round`: Media asks it who may add an upload to a round. `NewTicket\ScreenshotReportOnNewTicket` reads the new ticket form's posted screenshot fields (a shape it cannot read gets a plain message, never another exception; pins in the order of their posted number), checks the picture with `ReportPictures` and saves it through `createReport`; it fails with a `LogicException` when the request's tenant is not the organization it is given. `Component\ScreenshotReportField` is the form's Screenshot section: it posts `screenshot_media_id`, `screenshot_title`, `screenshot_summary` and `pins[n][x|y|note]`, and re-rendered after a refusal it keeps the title and summary, and the picture with its pins only when the picture is the logged-in person's own in their organization and not rejected (its row in [frontend.md](../../docs/02-architecture/frontend.md#design-system-twig-components); its browser side is the `screenshot-report` controller under [Stimulus controllers](../../docs/02-architecture/frontend.md#stimulus-controllers)).

Not built yet: checklist and review rounds, the draft round's UI, the ticket event on share, AI pins and the check items. See the phase 5 hand-off notes for what P05.T8 still lacks.
